# CorporateMapper — Project Context

CorporateMapper is a defensive cybersecurity / OSINT platform that maps an organisation's publicly visible attack surface.

The core concept is to discover and correlate publicly available information about an organisation, including:

- Employees
- Executives
- Departments
- Job roles
- Corporate hierarchy
- Domains and infrastructure
- Public technologies
- Public developer activity
- Vendors
- Contractors
- Partners
- Subsidiaries
- External corporate relationships

This information will eventually be represented as a central **Corporate Surface Map**, showing entities and the relationships between them.

The purpose is defensive: helping organisations understand what an external observer could learn about their people, infrastructure, organisational structure, and third-party relationships so they can reduce unnecessary exposure and better protect employees.
